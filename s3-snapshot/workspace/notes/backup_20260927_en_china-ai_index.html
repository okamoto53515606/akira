Chinese AI Models Overview [September 2026]

A permanent overview of major Chinese models — DeepSeek, Qwen (Alibaba), GLM (Zhipu/Z.ai), Kimi (Moonshot), MiniMax, StepFun and more. We prioritize primary sources (official announcements & pricing pages); where official figures are unavailable, third-party reporting/evaluations are clearly attributed. No speculation or rumors. See Pricing Comparison and the DeepSeek guide.

●Last updated: September 25, 2026 (info retrieved 2026-09-25; Kimi K3 pricing and Bedrock data handling verified and added) | Primary-source links per entry

Why track Chinese AI

Open weights and aggressive pricing give these models outsized reach in both API and self-hosted use.

  • Origin of the "DeepSeek Shock" — DeepSeek R1 (Jan 2025) shipped o1-class reasoning at low cost under an MIT license, resetting industry pricing and release strategy.
  • The open-weight front line — Qwen, GLM, Kimi, MiniMax and DeepSeek have all released flagship-class open models, many under MIT / Apache 2.0-style licenses.
  • Price competitiveness — DeepSeek's peak/off-peak model and low-cost Qwen/GLM flagships can cost a fraction of comparable Western models.
  • Caveats — Self-reported benchmarks can diverge from independent evaluations. Check data-sovereignty, regional and ToS considerations too.

Major labs & flagships

DeepSeek

DeepSeek (深度求索)

China's most prominent lab after the R1 "DeepSeek Shock." Current: V4.1 Flash (released 2026-09-10; 1M context, 384K output, native image input). V4 Flash and Vision Exp were consolidated into V4.1 Flash. V4 Pro keeps running after 9/14 (the 9/14 routing notice was withdrawn). MIT license. Peak/off-peak pricing since 2026-08-16. Guide · What changes · Pricing.

Qwen / Alibaba

Qwen (通义千问 / Alibaba Cloud)

Alibaba's open-weight family. Flagship Qwen3.8-Max (GA 2026-08-03) is a 2.4T-parameter MoE with 1M context. Low-cost Qwen3.8-Flash (2026-08-26) is a 125B/6B active MoE previewing the Qwen4 architecture (open-weight Flash-Next on HF/ModelScope; check each model card for license terms). Qwen3.8-Flash details.

GLM / Zhipu

GLM (Zhipu AI / Z.ai)

Tsinghua-affiliated lab. GLM-5.2 (2026-06-16) is a 744B MoE under MIT; GLM-5.3 (2026-08-14) ships at the same price. GLM-5.3-Flash (2026-08-26) is a 320B/18B MoE, natively multimodal, at list $0.15/$0.50. First foundation-model AI to IPO (Hong Kong, Jan 2026). GLM 5.3 Flash details.

Kimi / Moonshot

Kimi (Moonshot AI / 月之暗面)

Beijing startup. Kimi K3 (2026-07-16) is a 2.8T-parameter model with native vision and a 1M-token context, priced at $3.00 input / $15.00 output, with cached input at $0.30 (Moonshot direct API) — Claude Sonnet territory rather than the budget tier. It is also served through third-party hosts such as OpenRouter, and became available on Amazon Bedrock in September 2026 (AWS blog, 2026-09-18). K2.6 (Apr 2026) is reported to match GPT-5.5 on SWE-bench Pro.

Data handling via Bedrock (Bedrock-wide policy): The Kimi K3 model card itself has no data-handling section (verified 2026-09-25); what applies when you call Kimi K3 through Bedrock is Bedrock-wide policy. AWS's abuse-detection doc states that "Amazon Bedrock uses a zero operator access (ZOA) data security model. This means no operators of the service can access model input or output," and that it "uses a zero data retention (ZDR) data security model. This means that by default, Amazon Bedrock does not store model inputs or outputs." Kimi K3 is not on the list of models that require retention (OpenAI GPT-6 Astra family, Claude Fable 5, etc.). The data protection doc explains that because model providers cannot access the AWS-side Model Deployment Accounts, "they don't have access to Amazon Bedrock logs or to customer prompts and completions," and the AWS FAQ answers that inputs and outputs "are not shared with any model providers" and that neither AWS nor third-party model providers "will not use any inputs to or outputs from Amazon Bedrock to train Amazon Nova, Amazon Titan, or any third-party models." Customer content is encrypted and stored in the AWS Region you are using. Retention policy is configurable at the account or project level per Region; for guaranteed zero retention, set data_retention_mode: none (data retention). Kimi K3 model card / abuse detection (ZOA/ZDR) / data protection / AWS FAQ (verified 2026-09-25)

MiniMax

MiniMax

M3 (May/June 2026) is a multimodal MoE with 1M context. M2.7 open-sourced 2026-04-12. Low-cost, agent-oriented models.

StepFun

StepFun (阶跃星辰)

A Chinese AI startup. Its official documentation splits pricing by model category, and one of those categories is “Multimodal Reasoning Models”, where as of 2026-09-23 it lists step-5-preview ($1.00 / $2.70) and step-3.7-flash ($0.20 / $1.15), with cached input at $0.05 and $0.04 respectively. The wider catalogue spans text-only, vision, speech and image generation — for speech, stepaudio-2.5-realtime ($1.50/$10.00) and step-audio-r1.5 ($1.43/$15.00), among others. See the pricing split.

Others

ByteDance Doubao / Baidu ERNIE / Tencent Hunyuan

ByteDance's Doubao (豆包) is a leading consumer assistant (closed). Baidu's ERNIE 5.x is omnimodal (only ERNIE 4.5 is open-source). Tencent open-sourced Hunyuan-A13B.
