OpenAI's wording is that it is “reducing API prices for Sol and Luna by 50% compared with their GPT-5.6 promotional pricing” — these are replacements for the earlier models of the same name, not new names living alongside them.

The other headline: better prompt caching

Alongside the price cut, OpenAI describes caching improvements for GPT-6: 90% off cached input reads, plus better default cache hit rates.

FeatureFrom the announcement
MonitoringA Prompt Caching Dashboard shows how much of your input was cached over time.
DiagnosticsA diagnostics tool explains missed cache opportunities and points at fixes.
Change settings without breaking the cacheShifting reasoning effort or enabling/disabling tools keeps earlier context available for cache reuse.
Explicit breakpointsDevelopers can choose where a cached prefix ends.
Real-world impact (GitHub)GitHub reports that over the past few months it “reduced the share of prompt tokens requiring fresh processing by more than 50%” across billions of requests.

Changing effort without invalidating the cache fits agent loops that dial reasoning depth up and down. Background on how caching works is in our glossary entry on prompt caching.

Source: OpenAI, “Introducing GPT-6 Sol and Luna” (Improving caching section)

Alignment and writing style

  • Better than the GPT-5.6 versions: OpenAI says “both Sol and Luna show improvements over their GPT-5.6 counterparts, including lower rates of misleading claims about their coding work” — while noting that “the evaluations below deliberately test challenging situations and do not measure failure rates in typical use”.
  • System card: published at deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-astra — the Astra card also carries the safety evaluations for this generation.
  • Style inherited from Astra: “more clarity, less jargon, fewer odd turns of phrase, fewer low-value details, and slightly shorter answers overall without losing substance”, with the biggest gains in technical and coding conversations.

Source: OpenAI, “Introducing GPT-6 Sol and Luna” (Continuing to improve alignment, Collaboration style)

How to choose

  • Cost-first bulk processing: GPT-6 Luna at $0.10 input / $0.50 output is near the front of the low-price band — see how it compares on our pricing comparison.
  • Hard business tasks: GPT-6 Sol. OpenAI claims it beat Claude Opus 5 on AutomationBench at roughly one eleventh of the cost per task.
  • Luna is not weak: on DeepSWE 1.1, Luna (max) scores 66.6%, comparable to Claude Opus 5 and Fable 5 at medium effort. It responds to more test-time effort.
  • Watch for name confusion: check whether the URL is /en/gpt-5-6-sol/ or /en/gpt-5-6-luna/ (legacy) versus /en/gpt-6-sol-luna/ (current). Legacy pages keep the prices that were correct when published.
  • Peak accuracy: GPT-6 Astra remains the top model — see our GPT-6 Astra page.
