Trusted Access Programs

Access to Mythos 5.1 is rolling out through the verification programs below — LSVP is already underway, while CVP will add Mythos-class support soon.

🔐 Cyber Verification Program (CVP)

Currently provides access to certain Opus- and Sonnet-class models with reduced cyber safeguards for defensive security work. Mythos-class models will be added in the near future. Apply to join the CVP →

🧬 Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP)

Lets life-sciences professionals use Mythos 5.1 with safeguards designed for professional R&D (all other safeguards remain). Enrolled its first participants in partnership with the US government; plans to expand to the broader life-sciences community.

※ Currently limited to US organizations. Anthropic is coordinating with the US government to expand to a broader set of domestic and international partners.

Source: Anthropic announcement (Trusted access for Claude Mythos 5.1)
