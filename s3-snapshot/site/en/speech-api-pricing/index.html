Speech API Pricing [Nov 2026]

Scope: text-to-speech (TTS) and transcription (STT) rates, normalised to one minute and one hour. Vendors bill in four different units (per 1M characters, per 1M audio tokens, per minute, per hour), so the raw numbers are not comparable. Conversion assumptions are in the collapsed block below (official pages fetched 2026-11-15).

Bottom line (3 lines)

  • Japanese narration → MAI-Voice-2.1-Flash ($0.0045/min). OpenAI tts-1 costs the same but is deprecated, so it is not the current pick.
  • Transcription of recordings → MAI-Transcribe-2 ($0.10/hour), a promotional rate valid until the end of this year. Next is Meta Muse Voice Transcribe at $0.18/hour.
  • Realtime conversation → an order of magnitude more expensive per minute (from $0.023/min). Do not compare it in the same table as file transcription.

Caveats (4)

  • OpenAI's three character-billed TTS models (tts-1 / tts-1-hd / gpt-4o-mini-tts) are deprecated. They stay in the table for comparison, not as a recommendation.
  • "300 characters per minute" is this site's assumption (slow Japanese narration). English is roughly three times faster, so character-billed models consume more characters per minute.
  • Two Gemini TTS models double in price on 2027-01-01 (the right-hand figure in the table).
  • OpenAI does not publish a seconds-to-audio-token conversion for gpt-4o-mini-tts, so its per-minute figure is an estimate.
Conversion assumptions and the official wording (click to open)
  • Audio tokens: 25 tokens per second of audio (1,500 per minute) — Google's note
  • Characters to duration: 300 characters per minute (5 per second) — this site's assumption; vendors do not publish a speaking rate
  • 1,000 characters = 3 min 20 s (200 s); 1 hour = 18,000 characters — derived above
  • USD only, no FX conversion
Audio tokens correspond to 25 tokens per second of audio.
