Three things to take away

  • Cache read discounts span 4x across the five models. The deepest is Claude Fable 5.2 at 0.025x (97.5% off); Muse Wonder sits in the middle at 0.05x (95% off); the shallowest is 0.1x (90% off) on GPT-6 Astra and Kimi K3.1 Pro. For a workload that resends the same prefix, the multiplier can matter more than the headline input rate.
  • Discount rate and absolute price are different questions. The most expensive cached read here is GPT-6 Astra at $1.00; Fable 5.2, Kimi K3.1 Pro and Muse Wonder all sit at $0.20.
  • Choosing ZDR costs you features. Meta states that with ZDR enabled, server-side conversation state, background requests, file uploads, web search and Contributor mode are unavailable. Retention settings and feature settings are the same switch.
